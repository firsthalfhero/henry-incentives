How this works

Each activity has its own week. Violin, schoolwork and reading are tracked separately — extra reading doesn't make up for skipping violin.

The price climbs as you go — for each activity, your first day of the week pays the least, your seventh pays the most. Fresh start every week, no penalty for a quiet one.

Over $40 in a week? You still earn every dollar — anything past $40 is automatically set aside as savings rather than handed over as spending money.

One shared chart. This page has no memory of its own — every tap is saved straight away, so it looks the same on every device.

Getting paid

At the end of the week, show Mum or Dad your total to get paid — up to $40 as spending money, plus whatever's gone to savings.

Mum or Dad will reset the chart for the new week — or it resets itself automatically once the new week starts.
